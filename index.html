AI Engineer: Level Up

Ten levels. Build your character, earn new gear every time you clear one, and take the crown at the Capstone boss. Every run generates brand-new questions from random scenarios and shuffled answers — memorizing the answers will not save you.

  • 3 lives. A wrong answer or a timeout costs one.
  • Clear a level, pick a piece of gear — your character wears it for the rest of the run.
  • Answer fast for bonus points; build a streak for more.
  • From Level 4 on, earlier levels come back without warning.
  • Beat Level 10 to win the Capstone Crown and a printable certificate.
    Responsible AI is Level 6 — nobody reaches the crown without clearing it.
